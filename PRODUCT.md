# Makhafola & Associates Inc.

## Product purpose

Marketing website for a law firm of attorneys in Pretoria, South Africa. The site exists to make one decision easy: "Is this firm credible, relevant to my problem, and easy to reach?"

## Positioning

**A law firm that thinks like an engineer.**

The director, Matsobane Meshack Makhafola, is an admitted Attorney of the High Court of South Africa with 25+ years of senior IT experience (risk management, cybersecurity, cloud and IT operations at blue-chip corporations) and five university degrees spanning science, risk and law.

## Users

A Pretoria business owner or private individual with a legal problem, checking on a phone during a work break or on a laptop in the evening. They are anxious and want to know quickly whether the firm is credible, relevant and reachable.

**Scene sentence:** high-contrast, easy reading on light surfaces for long text; deep navy for brand moments (hero, question section, contact, footer); phone and WhatsApp always one tap away.

## Brand voice

Precise, calm, assured. Three words: **exact, unhurried, accountable**.

- Large confident serif type and generous space.
- Navy is the committed brand colour; gold is rare.
- Motion feels engineered: exact, quick, never bouncy.

## Mood references (do not copy)

- BLC & GSJ: large light serif headline on a dark field, question-style hook.
- CLOU architects: a confident paragraph as the hero statement, Swiss-style layout.

## Anti-references

Interchangeable law-firm sites: stock gavels, handshakes, scales and Lady Justice imagery, nine identical practice tiles, testimonials carousels, "award-winning" badges.

## Regulatory constraints (Legal Practice Council)

- No testimonials, client logos, case results, success rates, invented statistics or comparisons with other firms.
- No superlatives ("best", "leading", "No. 1").
- No gavels, scales or Lady Justice imagery (the scales already appear in the logo).

## Copy rules

- Every word earns its place. No intros that restate the heading.
- No em dashes.
- Banned words: streamline, empower, leverage, unleash, transform, seamless, world-class, cutting-edge, next-generation, game-changer, holistic, bespoke.
- Avoid the punchy "statement. Short negation." rhythm as a recurring voice.
- Button labels are verb + object. Link text makes sense on its own.
- Do not invent facts.
