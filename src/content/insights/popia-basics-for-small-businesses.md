The Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) applies to almost every business in South Africa that keeps records about people: customers, employees, suppliers or website visitors. Size does not exempt you.

## What counts as personal information

Names, ID numbers, contact details, account numbers, location data and opinions about a person are all personal information. So are records about a company, because POPIA protects juristic persons too.

## The conditions you must meet

POPIA sets out eight conditions for lawful processing. In practice, most small businesses need to:

- know what personal information they collect, where it is kept and who can see it;
- collect only what they need, for a purpose they can explain;
- tell people why their information is collected and how to contact you about it;
- keep it secure, and act quickly if it is lost or accessed without authority;
- delete or de-identify it when it is no longer needed.

## Your Information Officer

Every business has an Information Officer. By default it is the head of the business, such as the sole proprietor or the CEO. The Information Officer must be registered with the Information Regulator.

## Where to start

Begin with a simple record of the personal information you hold and why. That record makes the privacy notice, the security review and the retention schedule much easier to write.
