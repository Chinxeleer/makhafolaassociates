The Commission for Conciliation, Mediation and Arbitration (CCMA) resolves many workplace disputes in South Africa, including most unfair dismissal claims.

## Time limits matter

An unfair dismissal dispute must generally be referred to the CCMA within 30 days of the dismissal. Unfair labour practice disputes generally have a 90-day limit. A late referral needs an application for condonation, which is not granted automatically.

## Conciliation first

Most disputes start with conciliation: a commissioner helps the parties try to reach an agreement. If the matter does not settle, the commissioner issues a certificate, and the dispute may proceed to arbitration.

## Arbitration

At arbitration, each side presents evidence and the commissioner makes a binding award. Remedies for an unfair dismissal can include reinstatement or compensation.

## Representation

Representation by a legal practitioner is limited at some stages, particularly in arbitrations about dismissal for conduct or capacity, where the commissioner decides whether to allow it. Getting advice before the hearing is still valuable, even when you appear yourself.
